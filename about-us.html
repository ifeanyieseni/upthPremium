UPTH Premium is a state-of-the-art medical facility designed to deliver specialized, high-quality healthcare services within the University of Port Harcourt Teaching Hospital (UPTH). We offer expert-driven, patient-centric medical care across multiple specialties, ensuring world-class treatment in a modern, comfortable, and efficient setting.

As a premium extension of UPTH, our facility provides a private, efficient, and personalized healthcare experience, catering to patients who require specialized attention, shorter wait times, and superior medical expertise. Our mission is to bridge the gap between public and private healthcare by offering advanced medical services with a focus on excellence, innovation, and compassionate care.

Our Departments

General Consultation

At UPTH Premium, our General Consultation service provides comprehensive healthcare for individuals seeking a thorough assessment of their health concerns. Our dedicated team of experienced physicians offers professional consultations, ensuring that each patient receives personalized care tailored to their unique health needs.

Whether you're dealing with a common illness, need a routine check-up, or require advice on managing your health and wellness, our General Consultation department is here to help.

Surgery

Our Surgery Department offers a comprehensive range of surgical services, combining advanced techniques, state-of-the-art technology, and expert care to treat a variety of conditions. Whether you require minor outpatient surgery or a more complex procedure, our highly skilled surgical team ensures that you receive the best possible care from consultation through recovery.

Maternity

We are dedicated to providing exceptional care for expectant mothers and their newborns. From prenatal care through labor, delivery, and postnatal care, we prioritize the health and well-being of both mother and baby. Emergencies

Equipped to handle urgent medical situations and provide immediate, life-saving interventions when every second counts. Whether you're experiencing a medical emergency, injury, or sudden illness, our highly trained emergency physicians, nurses, and support staff are here to provide fast, effective care in a calm, compassionate environment. We are committed to ensuring that you receive the timely care you need, 24/7, 365 days a year.
