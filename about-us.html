Mission
  • To provide world-class medical services locally in Nigeria.
  • Ensure Nigerians no longer need to travel abroad for top-quality healthcare.
  • Deliver healthcare with the same level of expertise, technology, and professionalism found in developed countries.
  • Address diverse healthcare needs with precision, compassion, and professionalism.
Vision
  • To redefine healthcare delivery in Nigeria.
  • Establish UPTH Premium as a game-changing facility offering global-standard care.
  • Create a new era of healthcare built on excellence, innovation, and trust.
  • Be the preferred destination for advanced, specialized medical services in Port Harcourt and beyond.

Message from the CEO

At UPTH Premium, we are redefining healthcare delivery in Nigeria. This facility is a result of a strategic collaboration between the University of Port Harcourt and Patricare Hospital — an initiative rooted in a shared vision to offer worldclass medical care right here in Port Harcourt.

Our commitment is to provide medical services of global standards, ensuring that Nigerians no longer need to travel abroad for top-quality healthcare. Whether it's India, Europe, or any part of the developed world, the same level of expertise, technology, and professional care is now available locally.

UPTH Premium is truly world-class. Any medical service you would typically seek in India or Europe can now be accessed right here in Port Harcourt, with the same level of expertise and technology. This facility is a game-changer for healthcare in Nigeria.”

— Teinye Isokariari, CEO, UPTH Premium

Our Departments

General Consultation

At UPTH Premium, our General Consultation service provides comprehensive healthcare for individuals seeking a thorough assessment of their health concerns. Our dedicated team of experienced physicians offers professional consultations, ensuring that each patient receives personalized care tailored to their unique health needs.

Whether you're dealing with a common illness, need a routine check-up, or require advice on managing your health and wellness, our General Consultation department is here to help.

Surgery

Our Surgery Department offers a comprehensive range of surgical services, combining advanced techniques, state-of-the-art technology, and expert care to treat a variety of conditions. Whether you require minor outpatient surgery or a more complex procedure, our highly skilled surgical team ensures that you receive the best possible care from consultation through recovery.

Maternity

We are dedicated to providing exceptional care for expectant mothers and their newborns. From prenatal care through labor, delivery, and postnatal care, we prioritize the health and well-being of both mother and baby.
