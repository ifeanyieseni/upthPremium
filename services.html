Outpatient Clinics & Specialist Consultations

Our Outpatient Clinics provide patients with direct access to expert medical consultations across various specialties. Whether you need a routine check-up, a second opinion, or specialized care, our team of experienced consultants is available to evaluate, diagnose, and recommend personalized treatment plans.

What to Expect:

  • ✔ Short wait times and efficient appointment scheduling.
  • ✔ Expert consultations across all medical disciplines, including Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Endocrinology, Oncology, Urology, Nephrology, and more.
  • ✔ A patient-first approach, ensuring personalized care and attention.
  • ✔ Seamless referrals for diagnostics, procedures, or hospital admissions as required.
Surgeries & Procedures

At UPTH Premium, we offer advanced surgical care in a modern, well-equipped operating suite. Our team of highly skilled surgeons performs both major and minor procedures, utilizing the latest medical techniques to ensure the best outcomes.

Our Surgical Services Include:

  • ✔ General Surgery – Appendectomies, hernia repair, tumor excisions, etc.
  • ✔ Laparoscopic & Minimally Invasive Surgery – Faster recovery and reduced complications.
  • ✔ Orthopedic Surgery – Joint replacements, fracture repairs, and spine procedures.
  • ✔ Gynecological Surgery – Fibroid removal, hysterectomies, and fertility-related procedures.
  • ✔ Urological Surgery – Prostate procedures, kidney stone removal, and bladder surgeries.
  • ✔ Neurosurgery – Brain and spinal procedures with expert neurosurgical teams.
Maternity & Antenatal Care

UPTH Premium Maternity Services are designed to provide expecting mothers with high-quality, personalized care throughout their pregnancy journey. Our dedicated team of obstetricians, gynecologists, midwives, and neonatal specialists ensures a safe and comfortable childbirth experience.

Our Maternity Services Include:

  • ✔ Antenatal Clinics – Regular check-ups, fetal monitoring, and pregnancy support.
  • ✔ Labor & Delivery – Natural births, C-sections, and pain management options.
  • ✔ Postnatal Care – Support for new mothers and newborns, including breastfeeding guidance.
  • ✔ High-Risk Pregnancy Management – Specialized care for mothers with medical complications.
  • ✔ Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) – Specialized care for premature or high-risk newborns.
Diagnostics & Imaging

Accurate diagnosis is the foundation of effective medical treatment. At UPTH Premium, we offer advanced diagnostic and imaging services, ensuring that patients receive precise and timely results for informed medical decisions.

Our Diagnostic Services Include:

  • ✔ Ultrasound & Doppler Scans – Non-invasive imaging for pregnancy, organ health, and vascular conditions.
  • ✔ CT Scan & MRI – High-resolution imaging for detailed internal analysis.
  • ✔ Digital X-Ray – Quick and precise imaging for bone and chest conditions.
  • ✔ Mammography – Early breast cancer detection with advanced imaging.
  • ✔ Electrocardiogram (ECG) & Echocardiography – Heart function assessments for cardiac health.
  • ✔ Endoscopy & Colonoscopy – Minimally invasive procedures for digestive system evaluations.
Emergency & Critical Care

UPTH Premium provides 24/7 emergency and critical care services, ensuring rapid response and expert medical intervention in urgent situations. Our Emergency Unit is equipped with life-saving technology and experienced emergency care teams to manage all types of medical emergencies.

Our Emergency Services Cover:

  • ✔ Acute Medical & Trauma Care – Heart attacks, strokes, injuries, and severe infections.
  • ✔ Advanced Life Support & Resuscitation – On-site stabilization of critical patients.
  • ✔ Intensive Care Unit (ICU) & High Dependency Unit (HDU) – Specialized care for critically ill patients.
  • ✔ Emergency Surgeries – Immediate surgical intervention for life-threatening conditions.
  • ✔ Fast-Track Referral System – Seamless transfer for advanced care when necessary.
Pharmacy & Laboratory Services

At UPTH Premium, we offer a fully stocked pharmacy and a state-of-the-art laboratory to support all our medical services. Our goal is to provide quick and reliable access to medications and diagnostic tests in one location.

Pharmacy Services:

  • ✔ Certified, high-quality medications for various conditions.
  • ✔ Prescription fulfillment and medication counseling.
  • ✔ Specialized and hard-to-find medications available on request.

Laboratory Services:

  • ✔ Routine & Specialized Blood Tests – Comprehensive analysis for accurate diagnosis.
  • ✔ Microbiology & Infectious Disease Screening – Ensuring effective treatment for infections.
  • ✔ Hormone & Metabolic Testing – Essential evaluations for endocrine and metabolic disorders.
  • ✔ Cancer Screening & Pathology Services – Early detection and biopsy analysis.
